Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

The short version: Monk Fast doesn't collect, share or sell any of your data. Your fasts stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

Your fasts (start and end times, and your goal) and your settings are saved only on your device, in storage that the app and its widgets share. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

What we collect

Nothing. Monk Fast has no servers, no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no third-party SDKs.

Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. Widgets, the Live Activity and Control Center show information the app already has on your device. You can turn each of these off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you start or end a fast with Siri or Shortcuts, iOS handles the request and the app updates the fast on your device. We receive nothing.

Links to other websites

Articles in the Learn tab list their sources. Tapping a source opens it in your browser; those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Monk Fast isn't intended for children and collects no information from anyone.

Health information

Monk Fast provides general information about fasting. It is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Questions about privacy? See the support page.
