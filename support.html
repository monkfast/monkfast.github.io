Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

I forgot to start my fast. Can I fix it?
Yes. Tap I started earlier before starting, or Edit under Started while fasting, and pick the real start time. You can also edit or delete any past fast in History.

How do I get the timer on my Lock Screen?
Turn on Live Activity in the app's Settings, then start a fast.

I'm not getting reminders.
Check that notifications are allowed for Monk Fast in iOS Settings, and choose which reminders you want in the app under Settings → Notifications.

What is Monk mode?
A distraction-free view: just the timer and the one button you need. Turn it on in Settings.

Is this medical advice?
No. Monk Fast is general information and a timer. Talk to a doctor before fasting, especially if you're pregnant, have a health condition or take medication.
